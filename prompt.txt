bu adrese gidecek : https://imail.edu.vn/

daha sonra buraya tıklayıp domain uzantısı seçecek : <input x-ref="domain" type="text" class="block appearance-none bg-transparent border-0 w-full py-4 px-5 pr-8 cursor-pointer focus:outline-none select-none rounded-md placeholder-white placeholder-opacity-30" placeholder="Alan Adı Seç" name="domain" wire:model="domain" readonly="">


tikladigi yerden bu uzantiyi sececek : <a x-on:click="$refs.domain.value = 'imail.edu.vn'; $wire.setDomain('imail.edu.vn')" class="block px-4 py-2 text-sm leading-5 text-gray-700 cursor-pointer hover:bg-gray-100 focus:outline-none focus:bg-gray-100 transition duration-150 ease-in-out">imail.edu.vn</a>
